# Search Criteria

## Goal

Find 2-room apartments in Cluj-Napoca that are attractive strictly as buy-to-rent investments.

## Main criteria

- City: Cluj-Napoca
- Rooms: 2
- Preferred areas:
  - Gheorgheni
  - Mărăști
  - Între Lacuri
  - Zorilor
  - Mănăștur
  - Central
  - other areas only if the numbers are clearly better
- Target gross rental yield: >= 5.5%
- Preferred gross rental yield: >= 6%
- Calculate yield using realistic total acquisition cost, not only asking price
- Prefer easy-to-rent layouts and locations
- Avoid properties with large renovation requirements unless heavily discounted
- Verify the individual listing page is still active before considering it actionable

## Financial assumptions

For each listing estimate:

- asking price
- negotiated price
- price per square meter
- estimated realistic monthly rent
- gross yield
- expected acquisition/renovation/furnishing costs
- approximate mortgage payment
- likely monthly cash flow
- downside risks

## Negotiation rules

- Negotiate aggressively
- Do not ask only "care este ultimul preț?"
- Anchor below the price needed for an attractive investment return
- Never reveal maximum budget
- Never imply emotional attachment
- Walk away from poor economics
- Do not arrange a viewing until the negotiated price looks attractive

## Hard limits

Codex must never:

- commit to buying
- send money
- pay a deposit or reservation fee
- sign a contract
- share sensitive financial or personal information
- arrange an in-person viewing without explicit approval
